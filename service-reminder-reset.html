Service reminder reset (oil change and inspection) with VCDS

Reset the oil-change and inspection reminders in the instrument cluster with VCDS.

Tools needed
  • VCDS

Procedure

  1. Connect VCDS
    Use the car's OBD-II port.
  2. Turn on the ignition
    Leave the engine off.
  3. Click SRI Reset
    On the VCDS main screen. Not via 17 – Instruments → Adaptation.
  4. Wait for the service channels
    VCDS reads the cluster's service channels. Windows opening and closing on their own is normal.
  5. Choose the operation
    In the Operation drop-down, choose the applicable entry based on your region and work carried out.
  6. Click Perform SRI
    VCDS writes the channels and confirms the reset.
  7. Repeat for the other service
    If you did both an oil change and an inspection, repeat steps 5–6 with the other entry.

Results: Cycle the ignition and check that the instrument cluster shows a new service interval.
