Audi magnetic ride cancellation options

When you replace the factory magnetic ride ("Mag Ride") dampers with conventional dampers or coilovers, the damper control module (VCDS address 14, J250) loses the damper coils it expects to see. Left as is, this gives a damper warning, often an ESP warning and a dead ESP Off button. A lit ESP warning may fail a roadworthiness inspection. You therefore need some form of cancellation.
