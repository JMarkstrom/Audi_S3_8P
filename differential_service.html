Audi S3 8P

Differential service

Oil change for the front differential (transfer box) and the rear differential (final drive). The same steps apply to both units.

Shopping list
Tools needed
  • 5 mm Allen key
  • Fluid pump with hose
  • Floor jack
  • Jack stands
  • Torque wrench

Procedure

  1. Jack the car
    Support it on stands and make sure it is level.
  2. Undo the fill plug
    Remove the fill plug first so the unit can be refilled after draining.
  3. Undo the drain plug and let it drain
    Allow the oil to run until it slows to a drip.
  4. Install the drain plug
    Torque to 15 Nm.
  5. Fill until overflow
    Pump oil into the fill hole until it runs back out.
  6. Install the fill plug
    Torque to 15 Nm.

Note: Repeat the procedure for the front differential (transfer box) or the rear differential (final drive), depending on where you started.
