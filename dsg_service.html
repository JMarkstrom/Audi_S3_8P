DSG service

Oil and filter change for the DSG gearbox, filled to the overflow level at temperature.

Shopping list
Tools needed
  • VCDS
  • 14 mm Allen key
  • 24 mm hex socket
  • 8 mm Allen key
  • VAS 6262 or equivalent fill cap
  • Floor jack
  • Jack stands
  • Torque wrench

Procedure

  1. Jack the car
    Support it on stands and make sure it is level.
  2. Remove the intake and battery
  3. Replace the DSG filter with a new o-ring
    Torque to 20 Nm.
  4. Remove the DSG drain plug and drain the oil
    14 mm Allen key.
  5. Remove the overflow tube and drain the additional oil
    8 mm Allen key, inserted into the drain hole.
  6. Reinstall the overflow tube
    Torque to 3 Nm.
  7. Install the fill tool into the drain hole
    VAS 6262 or an equivalent aftermarket fill cap.
  8. Fill oil
    Approximately 5.5 litres.
  9. Start the engine
  10. Watch ATF temperature in VCDS
    Select Control Module > 02 – Auto Trans > Measurement Blocks, then select or type Group 019. Watch the ATF (Auto Trans Fluid) Temperature field.
  11. Cycle the selector while the temperature rises
    Sit in the driver’s seat. Hold the brake pedal, shift into each selector position and hold for about 3 seconds, then return the lever to Park.
  12. Disconnect the adapter when ATF is 35–45 °C
    Only then slide back under the car with the engine running and disconnect the adapter. Excess oil will drain. About ½ to ¾ litre should come out.
  13. Install the drain bolt with a new washer / gasket
    Torque to 45 Nm.
  14. Turn the engine off
