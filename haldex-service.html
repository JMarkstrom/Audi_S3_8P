Audi S3 8P

Haldex service (with pump replacement)

Oil, filter and optional pump replacement for the Haldex coupling.

Shopping list
Tools needed
  • 4 mm Allen key
  • 5 mm Allen key
  • 8 mm Allen key
  • Fluid pump with hose
  • Floor jack
  • Jack stands
  • Torque wrench
  • VCDS

Procedure

  1. Jack the car
    Support it on stands and make sure it is level.
  2. Remove the fill plug
    5 mm Allen key. Open the fill plug first so the unit can be refilled after draining.
  3. Remove the drain plug and let it drain
    8 mm Allen key. Allow the oil to run until it slows to a drip.
  4. Remove the Haldex filter bolts
    Two bolts, 4 mm Allen key.
  5. Lubricate the new filter with Haldex oil and install it
    Torque to 5 Nm.
  6. Remove and replace the Haldex pump
    Torque to 6 Nm.
  7. Install the drain plug
    Torque to 30 Nm.
  8. Fill Haldex oil until overflow
    Pump oil into the fill hole until it runs back out.
  9. Install the old fill plug
    Do not torque it.
  10. Prime the pump in VCDS
    Control module > AWD > Output tests.
  11. Fill additional Haldex oil until overflow
  12. Run the car to reach oil temperature
    20–40 °C.
  13. Fill additional Haldex oil until overflow
  14. Install the new fill plug
    Torque to 15 Nm.
  15. Lower the car
